DIGITAL WORKFLOW

From scan to smile, in four steps.

Every restoration follows the same digital chain — data in, design, manufacturing, and clinical delivery.

  1. 01

    Data Acquisition

    Digital intraoral scans or conventional impressions are received and converted into an accurate digital model.

  2. 02

    Digital Design

    Restorations are designed in CAD with attention to occlusion, contact points and aesthetic detail.

  3. 03

    Digital Manufacturing

    Milling and 3D printing turn the design into a physical restoration, supported by automated production lines.

  4. 04

    Clinical Delivery

    Each finished piece is inspected before shipping, then tried in at the clinic for fit and occlusion.
